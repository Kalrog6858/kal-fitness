# Kal Fit 💪

A personal workout app that runs in your phone's browser. It has animated exercise demos, set and rep tracking, rest timers, progress charts, and a guided daily pelvic floor routine.

## Weekly plan

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Cardio & Recovery: incline walk 30–40 min, glute bridges |
| Tue | Cardio & Recovery |
| **Wed (night)** | **Upper A**: chest, back, shoulders, arms, abs |
| Thu | Cardio & Recovery |
| **Fri** | **Lower A**: knee-safe legs, hamstrings, calves, abs, then a walk |
| **Sat** | **Upper B**: upper chest, lats, shoulders, arms, abs |
| **Sun** | **Lower B**: glutes, knee-safe legs, core, then a walk |
| Every day | Pelvic Floor & Stamina: 3 guided sessions of about 5 min |

Leg days use knee-friendly choices. There are no deep squats, lunges, jumping or twisting. The leg press and box squat stop at 90° of knee bend.

## Features
- An animated figure for every exercise, with the working muscles shown in orange
- Each set logs weight × reps. Your last workout's numbers are filled in, and the app tells you when it's time to add weight
- A rest timer that beeps and vibrates, plus a hold timer for planks
- Charts for body weight, waist, strength and consistency, plus a list of personal bests
- A guided pelvic floor session: squeeze, relax, quick flicks, relax breathing
- Works offline once it has been opened. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app

## Open it on your phone
1. In this repo, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, Branch to **main** and the folder to **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
3. After about a minute it's live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/kal-fitness/`.
4. Open that link on your phone and add it to your home screen:
   - iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen
   - Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Install app

## Your data
Your workout log is stored **only on your phone**, in the browser's storage. Nothing is uploaded to this repo. Use **More → Export** now and then to save a backup file.
